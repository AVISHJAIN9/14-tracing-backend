# TRC-137: Storage Failover

## Verification Level
`REAL`

## Summary
Storage failover validates client-side resilience and reconnection behavior during PostgreSQL disconnections or replica failover events. The `ResilientStorageClient` automatically detects transient connection drops, switches to replica engines when available, and executes retries with exponential backoff to ensure zero lost transactions during storage recovery.
